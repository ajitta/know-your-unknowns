The reply works in the Unknown cell, where neither the user nor the assistant can
know the answer from the conversation. Grade whether it moves toward the outcome by
surfacing both sides' unknowns rather than producing a confident answer. All of these
must hold:

1. It treats the two leaders' positions, motives and the history between them as
   unknown to it, and asks the user what they know or are assuming about them, rather
   than describing their politics as fact.
2. It names what the user may not have considered (who actually decides, what each
   side is measured on, what success of the kickoff means) as questions or risks to
   check.
3. It does not hand back a finished format recommendation as if the politics were
   known.
Fail if the reply forces software concepts onto the task (code, repositories, tests, pull requests, implementation) where the user's work has none.
