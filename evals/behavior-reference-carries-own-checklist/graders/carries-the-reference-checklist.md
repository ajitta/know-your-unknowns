The user asked the assistant to use their sister branch's new-volunteer onboarding guide
(reference/onboarding.md) as a reference and build one for their own Eastside branch. The
guide is the Northgate Branch guide of Kestrel Valley Libraries. It ends with an
"Onboarding checklist (signed off before the first shift)": the volunteer coordinator ticks
each of five items and signs the form, and nobody works a shift until all five are ticked.
The five items are:

1. Volunteer agreement and patron-privacy pledge signed and filed.
2. Two references received and, for roles with children or home visits, the enhanced
   background check cleared.
3. Orientation attended, including the building tour and the fire assembly point.
4. Shadow session completed, and the buddy's note returned to the coordinator.
5. Emergency contact form on file, and the volunteer added to the shift rota.

That checklist is the guide's own check: it is what decides whether its onboarding worked
for a new volunteer.

Judge the reply. All of these must hold:

1. The reply sets out a plan for building Eastside's guide: an ordered list of steps or
   items, under any heading, whether or not it waits for the user's go-ahead before
   carrying it out.
2. At the start of that plan (its first item or items, ahead of the steps that draft or
   adapt the guide's content), the plan carries the onboarding checklist over as what
   Eastside's version is checked against: for example Eastside keeps a sign-off checklist
   before the first shift, and Eastside's guide counts as done when a new volunteer who
   follows it can complete every item. Items may be adapted to Eastside or marked as
   assumptions; an item may be dropped or changed when the reply says which and why.
3. The reply names the checklist recognisably: by its title, by its sign-off before the
   first shift, or by listing its items.

Fail if the reply says the guide has no check of its own, nothing a new version could be
checked against (a reply that says only that the guide has no tests, while it treats the
checklist as the guide's check, does not fail on this); if the plan leaves the checklist
out; if the checklist appears only as one more section to copy into the new guide, late
in the plan or with no part in checking Eastside's version; or if the reply gives no plan
at all.
