# Elmfield Winter Fair

**Saturday 12 December 2026, 11am to 3pm**
Elmfield Primary School, hall and playground

Join us for the Elmfield Winter Fair! Craft stalls, a cake sale, a tombola, face
painting and hot chocolate.

Every penny raised goes to our new school library.

Free entry. Everyone welcome.

Want a stall at the Winter Fair? Tables are £10. Book with the school office by
1 December.
