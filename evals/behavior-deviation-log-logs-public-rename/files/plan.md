# Winter Fair 2026 — plan

Organised by Friends of Elmfield, the parents' association of Elmfield Primary School.
Agreed at the committee meeting on 14 October 2026.

## Outcome

Raise £2,500 towards the new school library, at an event that families from every year
group come to.

## Done when

- The fair runs on Saturday 12 December 2026, 11:00-15:00, in the school hall and playground.
- At least 12 stalls are booked by 1 December.
- Takings of £2,500 or more are counted and banked by 18 December.

## Decisions

1. Name: "Winter Fair". The invitations, the newsletter and the flyer all use it.
2. Invitations: emailed to all 300 families on the school's parent email list on
   1 November 2026, under the name "Winter Fair". Sent.
3. Hot chocolate: run by Bean & Co café, which keeps the takings and gives us 20%.
   *Superseded 2026-11-04, see DEVIATIONS.md.*
4. Flyer: one A5 flyer, 400 copies, to the printer by 20 November; given out in book
   bags and put up in the shops on the high street.
5. Stalls: crafts, cake sale, tombola, face painting, hot chocolate. Stallholders pay
   £10 a table.
