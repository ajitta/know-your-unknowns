Use this as a reference for our own volunteer policy. It's the neighbouring town's
library volunteer policy (excerpt):

1. Volunteers must be 16 or older; 14-15 with a guardian's consent form.
2. Every volunteer completes a 2-hour orientation before their first shift.
3. Volunteers may not handle patron records or cash.
4. A volunteer who misses three shifts without notice is removed from the roster.
5. The Friends of the Library committee reviews this policy every two years.

We are a smaller library with one paid staff member.
