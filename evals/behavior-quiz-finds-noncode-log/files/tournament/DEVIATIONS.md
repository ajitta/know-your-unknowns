# Deviation log — Under-12s tournament travel pack

## [2027-04-06] Travel

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: of the parents who offered to drive, only one holds the D1 category on their driving licence, which a 17-seat minibus needs. Eastbrook Community Transport lends the minibuses without drivers.
- **Deviation from plan**: "two 17-seat minibuses borrowed from Eastbrook Community Transport, driven by two volunteer parents".
- **Response chosen**: a 33-seat coach with its own driver, hired from Coastway Coaches for £364. The travel pack gives the cost to families as £26 per player instead of £18.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: one vehicle with a professional driver carries the whole squad and the staff together, and no parent needs a licence category they do not hold.
- **Alternatives considered**: one minibus plus parents' cars (booster seats and insurance checks for seven children, and the club's welfare policy asks for two adults in every car); one minibus making two trips (the second trip would arrive after the first game).
- **Risk/follow-up check**: families were told £18 at the March meeting. Only the committee can decide whether the club covers the £8 difference for families who cannot pay it.

## [2027-04-20] Overnight stay

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: Westcliff Youth Hostel cancelled the booking because it is closed for repairs in May. No other place within ten miles has beds for 17 people at the club's budget that weekend.
- **Deviation from plan**: "Overnight: Saturday night at Westcliff Youth Hostel" and "the knockout games on Sunday for the teams that qualify".
- **Response chosen**: a day trip on Saturday 15 May. The squad plays the group games only and has told the organisers it withdraws from Sunday's knockout games, even if it qualifies. The coach is back at the clubhouse by 19:30 on Saturday.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: going home on Saturday night and out again on Sunday morning would double the coach cost (another £364) and mean a 06:00 start for the players on Sunday.
- **Alternatives considered**: a second coach day for Sunday (£26 more per player); families drive their own children on Sunday (no welfare officer with them, and not every family has a car).
- **Risk/follow-up check**: players and parents expect a weekend and a chance at the knockout games, and the squad gives up its place in them even if it wins its group. Only the committee can say whether a day trip still meets the outcome of a first weekend tournament away from home.
