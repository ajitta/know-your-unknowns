# Westcliff Junior Cup: travel pack for Under-12s families

Eastbrook Youth FC. Saturday 15 May 2027, Westcliff Sports Park.

## 1. Getting there and back

- The squad travels by coach (Coastway Coaches, 33 seats, with its own driver).
- Meet at the clubhouse at 07:30; the coach leaves at 07:45.
- The coach leaves Westcliff at 17:45 and is back at the clubhouse by 19:30. Please collect your child from the clubhouse. A child goes home only with an adult named on their consent form.

## 2. Matches

- Group games at 10:30, 12:15, 14:00 and 15:30. Pitch numbers are given out on the day.
- Every player plays at least half of every game.
- The squad does not take part in Sunday's knockout games.

## 3. Cost

£26 per player, paid to the club treasurer by 1 May, by bank transfer or in cash at training. The club pays the entry fee. Tell the welfare officer if the cost is a problem: nobody is left out because of money.

## 4. What to bring

- Home and away kit, shin pads, boots and trainers (some pitches are artificial grass).
- A packed lunch, snacks and a one-litre water bottle. The sports park has a café.
- A waterproof jacket and a warm top.
- Any medicines, in a bag labelled with your child's name, handed to the welfare officer at the coach.

## 5. Consent and medical form

The consent and medical form must reach the welfare officer by 1 May. A player with no form does not travel.

## 6. On the day

- Staff: two coaches, Sam Okoro and Leah Brandt, and the welfare officer, Priya Nair, who carries the first-aid kit and the medicines.
- Contact during the day: Priya Nair, 07700 900123.
- If a player is hurt, the welfare officer decides with the tournament's first-aiders whether they keep playing, and phones the parent straight away.
