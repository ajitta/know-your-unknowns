# Under-12s away tournament: plan

Approved by the Eastbrook Youth FC committee on 3 March 2027.

Event: the Westcliff Junior Cup at Westcliff Sports Park, Saturday 15 and Sunday 16 May 2027.
Squad: 14 players, two coaches and the club's welfare officer.

Outcome: the Under-12s get their first weekend tournament away from home, and every player gets game time.

What we will do:

- Travel: two 17-seat minibuses borrowed from Eastbrook Community Transport, driven by two volunteer parents, leaving the clubhouse at 07:45 on Saturday 15 May.
- Overnight: Saturday night at Westcliff Youth Hostel (one dormitory for the players, two rooms for the staff).
- Matches: the group games on Saturday; the knockout games on Sunday for the teams that qualify.
- Return: Sunday 16 May, back at the clubhouse by 18:00.
- Cost to families: £18 per player, for the minibus fuel and the community transport fee. The club pays the entry fee (£150) and the hostel.
- Every player plays at least half of every game.
