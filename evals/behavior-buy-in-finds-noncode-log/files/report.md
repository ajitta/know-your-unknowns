# Free Sunday Evenings: pilot results

Twelve free Sunday evenings at Eastgate Town Museum, 7 June to 23 August 2026.

| Measure | Target | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Visitors, all twelve evenings | 1,800 | 2,124 |
| Average visitors per evening | 150 | 177 |
| First-time visitors (door survey, 702 responses) | 40% | 47% |
| Total cost | £14,400 | £13,310 |

Who came: 38% of survey respondents were aged 18-34, against 12% of our daytime visitors. 29% came as a family with children.

Busiest evening: 19 July, 241 visitors, during the town festival. Quietest: 9 August, 118 visitors, in heavy rain.

From the visitor book: "Finally something to do on a Sunday that does not cost anything."

Recommendation: fund year two, 40 Sunday evenings from March 2027, at £44,000.
