# Deviation log — Free Sunday Evenings pilot

## [2026-05-14] Opening hours

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: the bus company's new Sunday timetable, from 1 June, ends the last service from the museum stop at 20:25. Five of the eight evening staff go home on that bus.
- **Deviation from plan**: "Free entry every Sunday evening, 18:00-21:00".
- **Response chosen**: open 17:00-20:00 on all twelve evenings. The posters, the website and the press release give 17:00-20:00.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: staff who leave at 20:10 can catch the last bus, and the evening still runs three hours.
- **Alternatives considered**: keep 18:00-21:00 and pay taxis home for five staff (about £110 per evening, not in the budget); staff the evenings only with people who drive (three people, too few to open the ground floor safely).
- **Risk/follow-up check**: 17:00 is earlier than the after-work evening the plan was built on, so the pilot tested a 17:00-20:00 opening, not the one the board approved. The door survey did not record arrival times, so it cannot show whether people who work late were missed. Only the board can judge whether year two opens at 17:00 or pays for later hours.

## [2026-05-28] Family workshop

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: the education officer leaves on 31 May, and the post stays vacant until October.
- **Deviation from plan**: "A free drop-in family workshop, run by the education officer, from 18:00 to 19:00 each evening."
- **Response chosen**: no workshop on any of the twelve evenings. The front desk hands families a self-guided trail sheet instead.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: no one else on the staff holds the safeguarding training the museum's policy requires to lead a session with children.
- **Alternatives considered**: book a freelance facilitator (£180 per evening, with no budget line for it); run the workshop monthly with a volunteer (no volunteer holds the safeguarding training).
- **Risk/follow-up check**: families were one of the audiences the plan aimed at, and the pilot ran without the activity meant to bring them. The workshop's materials and overtime, about £90 per evening, were never spent, so the pilot's cost is lower than a year two with the workshop would be.
