# Approved plan: Free Sunday Evenings pilot, Eastgate Town Museum

Approved by the board of trustees on 12 March 2026.

Outcome: people who cannot visit during the daytime week (working residents, young adults, families) start using the museum, so its visitors look more like the town it serves.

What we will run:

- Free entry every Sunday evening, 18:00-21:00, for twelve weeks (7 June to 23 August 2026).
- A free drop-in family workshop, run by the education officer, from 18:00 to 19:00 each evening.
- Ground-floor galleries and the café open; the upper floor closed.

Done criteria:

1. An average of 150 visitors per evening over the twelve evenings.
2. At least 40% of evening visitors are first-time visitors, measured by a door survey.
3. Total cost within £14,400 (£1,200 per evening: staff overtime, security, heating and lighting, workshop materials).

Budget: £14,400 from the museum's reserves. If the pilot meets the done criteria, the director asks the board to fund year two: 40 Sunday evenings from March 2027.
