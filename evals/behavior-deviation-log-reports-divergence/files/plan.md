# Plan: Summer Reading Challenge 2026, Millbrook Library

Outcome: children aged 4-11 who use Millbrook Library keep reading through the school summer holidays, and their families come back to the library in September.

Done criteria:

1. 200 children signed up by 31 July.
2. 120 of them finish (six books read) by 31 August.

Decisions:

1. Launch day: 1 July 2026. Published to families in both primary schools' May newsletters and on the library website. *(Superseded 2026-05-20: see DEVIATIONS.md.)*
2. The challenge runs from launch day to 31 August. Each child reads six books and collects a sticker for each one at the front desk.
3. Prize budget: £600 from the Friends of the Library: a £4 book token for each finisher (up to 130 finishers, £520) and a prize draw of four £20 book tokens (£80). *(Superseded 2026-06-03: see DEVIATIONS.md.)*
4. Story session every Tuesday at 10:30 in the meeting room, which seats 40. Families book a place at the front desk.
5. Sign-up on a paper sheet at the front desk, typed into the programme spreadsheet each Friday.
6. Posters and flyers in the library's house style, up in both schools and the library by 15 June. *(Superseded 2026-05-12: see DEVIATIONS.md.)*
7. Six volunteers on a rota staff the sticker table during opening hours.
