# Deviation log — Summer Reading Challenge 2026

## [2026-05-06] Sign-up sheet

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: volunteers trying the sheet at the front desk found its column headings unclear.
- **Deviation from plan**: not covered (the plan says only "a paper sheet at the front desk").
- **Response chosen**: renamed the columns "Child's name" to "Reader's name", "Phone" to "Parent or carer phone" and "Books" to "Stickers". The columns hold the same information as before.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: the volunteers asked for headings a child's parent can read at a glance.
- **Alternatives considered**: keep the old headings and brief each volunteer *(inferred, not stated)*: loses because new volunteers join through the summer.
- **Risk/follow-up check**: whoever types the sheet into the spreadsheet on Fridays needs the new names. Nothing changes for families.

## [2026-05-12] Poster font

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: the house-style font, Gill Sans, is not installed on the branch computer that prints the posters.
- **Deviation from plan**: "Posters and flyers in the library's house style".
- **Response chosen**: posters and flyers set in Arial. Colours, logo and wording follow the house style.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: Arial is on every library computer and reads well at poster size.
- **Alternatives considered**: buy a Gill Sans licence (£35, not budgeted); have the central office print them (a two-week queue, which would miss 15 June).
- **Risk/follow-up check**: central marketing may ask for the house font next year. Nothing changes for families.

## [2026-05-20] Launch date

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: the supplier moved delivery of the reading packs (the booklet and sticker sheet each child takes home) from 24 June to 6 July.
- **Deviation from plan**: "Launch day: 1 July 2026. Published to families in both primary schools' May newsletters and on the library website."
- **Response chosen**: launch on 8 July. The library website now says 8 July, and both schools were asked to print the new date in their June newsletters.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: a child who signs up without a pack goes home with nothing, and the family has to come back to collect it.
- **Alternatives considered**: launch on 1 July and hand out packs from 8 July (two trips for every family); print packs locally (about £300 the programme does not have).
- **Risk/follow-up check**: families who read the May newsletters may arrive on 1 July. Only you know whether both schools printed the correction, and whether to put a volunteer at the door on 1 July.

**Discovery** [2026-05-27] Meeting room: the plan assumed it seats 40 / the fire notice on the door limits it to 30.

## [2026-06-03] Prize budget

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: the Friends of the Library approved £400 for prizes, not the £600 the plan assumed.
- **Deviation from plan**: "Prize budget: £600 from the Friends of the Library: a £4 book token for each finisher (up to 130 finishers, £520) and a prize draw of four £20 book tokens (£80)."
- **Response chosen**: a £400 budget. Each finisher gets a certificate and a medal (£2.40 each, up to 130 finishers, £312), and the prize draw keeps its four £20 book tokens (£80): £392 in all.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: it keeps a prize for every finisher, which the flyer promises ("every child who finishes wins a prize").
- **Alternatives considered**: keep the £4 book tokens and drop the prize draw (£400 covers 100 finishers, and the plan expects up to 130); ask the council for the missing £200 (no grant round before September).
- **Risk/follow-up check**: last year every finisher got a book token, so families may expect one again. Only you can judge whether a medal will disappoint them, and whether to ask the Friends to top the budget up.
