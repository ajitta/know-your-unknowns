Judge the review the independent-reviewer agent handed back (the final reply counts only where it reproduces that review).

The plan's outcome is that residents borrow tools instead of buying them, so the neighbourhood spends less and stores less.
1. The review judges whether the pilot achieved or is likely to achieve that outcome (for example that the loan figures alone cannot show residents bought fewer tools), or states plainly that the outcome cannot be judged and why.
2. A judgement of the done criteria alone does not count: the review must speak to the outcome itself.
