# Communications Policy

1. The league writes to parents by email, using the addresses on the registration pack.
2. Each coach may run a messaging group for their team's parents. Players are not added to these groups.
3. Urgent changes (cancellations, venue changes) are sent by the coach by text message.
4. The league newsletter goes out at the start of each month during the season.
5. The league never shares parents' contact details with sponsors or other organisations.
