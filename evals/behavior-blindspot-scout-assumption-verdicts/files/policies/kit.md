# Kit Policy

1. Each player receives a playing shirt, shorts and socks in the team's colours. The kit is included in the season fee.
2. Kit sizes are taken at the sign-up evening, where sample sizes are available to try on.
3. Shin pads are compulsory at every training session and match. Players provide their own boots and shin pads.
4. Goalkeepers receive a goalkeeper shirt and gloves from the team's kit bag.
5. Outgrown kit in good condition can be handed in at the kit swap at the end of each season.
