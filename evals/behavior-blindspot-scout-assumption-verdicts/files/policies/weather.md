# Weather and Cancellations Policy

1. When the weather is poor, pitches are inspected by 8:00 on match days. The fixtures secretary decides whether matches go ahead.
2. Coaches tell their team's parents by text message by 8:30 if a match is called off.
3. Matches stop during lightning and do not restart until 30 minutes after the last strike.
4. Training on frozen or waterlogged pitches is cancelled; the coach decides on the day.
