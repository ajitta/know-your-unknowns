# Volunteers Policy

1. The league is run entirely by volunteers: the committee, coaches, team managers, drivers, the kit organiser and the media volunteer.
2. A committee member meets every new volunteer and gives them a role description.
3. Volunteers who work with players hold a current criminal record check (see the Coaches Policy).
4. Volunteers agree to the Code of Conduct when they start and again each season.
5. Volunteers' contact details are kept by the league secretary and shared only with the people they work with.
