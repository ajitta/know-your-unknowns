# Insurance Policy

Owner: league secretary. Last reviewed: May 2025.

1. The league holds personal accident and public liability cover for registered players, coaches and volunteers during league training, matches and travel the league arranges.
2. Players under 16 are covered only once a waiver signed on paper by a parent or guardian is on file with the league secretary. Until it is, the player is not insured and must not train or play.
3. Players aged 16 and over are covered from the day their registration is complete.
4. Injuries are reported to the welfare officer on the accident report form within 7 days (see the Medical Policy); a claim cannot be made without it.
5. The cover renews on 1 August each year. The league secretary holds the policy schedule and the insurer's contact details.
