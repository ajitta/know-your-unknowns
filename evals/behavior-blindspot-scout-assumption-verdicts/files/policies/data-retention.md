# Data Retention Policy

Owner: league secretary. Last reviewed: September 2019.

1. Registration packs are kept in the locked cabinet in the clubhouse for the current season and the following season, then shredded.
2. Accident report forms are kept until the player turns 21.
3. Safeguarding concern records are kept by the welfare officer and are destroyed only with the welfare officer's agreement.
4. Team contact sheets held by coaches are returned to the league secretary at the end of each season and shredded.
5. Financial records (fees, refunds, hardship awards) are kept for six years.
