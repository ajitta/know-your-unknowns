# Coaches Policy

1. Every team has a head coach who holds at least an introductory coaching qualification, or completes one within the season.
2. Every coach and team manager holds a current criminal record check, renewed every three years, before working with players.
3. Coaches complete safeguarding training and a first aid course before the season starts.
4. Training runs with at least one adult for every eight players at Under-10 and younger, and one for every ten above that.
5. Coaches keep the team list and emergency contacts with them at every session.
