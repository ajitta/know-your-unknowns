# Refunds Policy

1. A family that withdraws a player before 30 September receives the season fee back, less a £10 administration charge.
2. After 30 September, refunds are given only on medical grounds, for the part of the season the player misses, and need a note from a doctor.
3. Kit that has been issued is not refunded.
4. Refunds are paid by the same method the fee was paid.
5. Requests go to the treasurer in writing.
