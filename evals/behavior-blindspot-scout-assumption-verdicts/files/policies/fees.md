# Fees Policy

1. The season fee is £120 per player, including kit. A second child in the same family pays £100, and a third or further child £80.
2. Fees are paid at the sign-up evening by cash, cheque or card, or by bank transfer within 14 days.
3. Families who cannot pay can ask the treasurer in confidence for help from the hardship fund. Nobody else is told.
4. A player whose fee is unpaid by 30 September is stood down from matches until it is paid or a hardship arrangement is agreed.
