# Photography and Media Policy

1. Parents choose a photo preference at registration: photos allowed, team photos only, or no photos.
2. Before posting any photo, the league's media volunteer checks each player shown against the photo preference list.
3. Players are never named in a caption with their full name.
4. Photos are posted only on the league's own website and social media accounts.
5. A parent can change the photo preference at any time by telling the registration secretary.
