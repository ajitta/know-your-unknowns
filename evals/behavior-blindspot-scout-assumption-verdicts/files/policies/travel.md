# Travel Policy

1. Parents are responsible for getting their child to home fixtures and training.
2. For away fixtures, the league arranges a minibus for Under-12 and older teams when more than eight players need transport. Other players travel with their own parents or in cars arranged between parents.
3. Minibus drivers are league volunteers aged 25 or over with a current criminal record check and the right licence category.
4. A player travels in league-arranged transport only if their parental consent form for the season is in place.
5. Coaches carry the team list with emergency contact numbers on every journey.
