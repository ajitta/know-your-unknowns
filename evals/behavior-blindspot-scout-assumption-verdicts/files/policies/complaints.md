# Complaints Policy

1. A complaint is raised first with the team coach or manager, who aims to resolve it within 7 days.
2. If it is not resolved, the complaint goes in writing to the league secretary, who acknowledges it within 5 days and answers within 21 days.
3. A complaint about a safeguarding matter goes straight to the welfare officer.
4. If the person is still not satisfied, the committee hears an appeal at its next meeting. The committee's decision is final.
