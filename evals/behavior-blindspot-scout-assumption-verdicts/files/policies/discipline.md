# Discipline Policy

1. A player sent off misses their team's next match. Five yellow cards in a season bring a one-match suspension.
2. The fixtures secretary records suspensions and sends them to the team manager by email.
3. Breaches of the Code of Conduct by parents or spectators are reviewed by the committee, which may give a warning or ban the person from the touchline.
4. Any fine the county association gives for a team's conduct is passed on to that team.
