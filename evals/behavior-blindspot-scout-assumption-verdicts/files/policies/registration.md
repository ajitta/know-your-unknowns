# Registration Policy

Applies to every player, Under-7 to Under-18. Owner: registration secretary.

1. Registration for each season opens at the sign-up evening in late July and closes on 31 August. Late registrations are taken until 30 September where a team has space.
2. For each child, a parent or guardian completes the registration pack: player details, the parental consent form, the medical information sheet and the photo preference.
3. The registration secretary checks the pack against the player's proof of age (birth certificate or passport, seen in person) before adding the player to a team list.
4. A player may not train or play until registration is complete and the season fee is paid or a hardship arrangement is agreed (see the Fees Policy).
5. Returning players complete a new pack every season. Nothing is carried over from the previous season.
6. Players aged 16 and 17 complete their own registration pack, but the parental consent form still needs a parent or guardian.
