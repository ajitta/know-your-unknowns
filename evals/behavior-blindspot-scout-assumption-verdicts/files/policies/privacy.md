# Privacy Policy

Owner: league secretary, who is also the data protection lead.

1. The league collects only what it needs to run the league: player and parent names, dates of birth, addresses, contact details, medical information, photo preferences and fee records.
2. Personal information is seen only by the committee members and coaches who need it for their role.
3. Parents can ask the league secretary to see the information held about them or their child; the league answers within one month.
4. Medical information is shared only with the player's coach and, in an emergency, with medical staff.
5. How long each record is kept is set out in the Data Retention Policy.
