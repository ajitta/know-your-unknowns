# Safeguarding Policy

Owner: welfare officer.

1. The league has a named welfare officer. Parents, players and volunteers can raise a concern with the welfare officer in person, by phone or by email.
2. Every adult who coaches, manages a team or drives players holds a current criminal record check before they start (see the Coaches Policy).
3. Two adults are present at every session and on every journey. No adult is alone with a player.
4. Concerns are reported to the welfare officer within 24 hours and recorded on the concern form. The welfare officer decides on referral.
5. Concern records are confidential and are kept as the Data Retention Policy sets out.
