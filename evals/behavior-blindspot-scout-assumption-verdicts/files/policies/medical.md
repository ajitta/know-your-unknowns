# Medical Policy

1. Parents complete a medical information sheet for each child at registration: conditions, allergies, medication and an emergency contact.
2. The coach keeps a copy of each player's sheet in the team's first aid bag.
3. Every team has a first aid kit at every session; the team manager checks it monthly.
4. Any injury is recorded on the accident report form on the day and sent to the welfare officer within 7 days.
5. A player with a suspected head injury stops playing at once and does not return to football until cleared under the league's concussion guidance.
