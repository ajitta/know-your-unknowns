# Age Groups Policy

1. Teams run from Under-7 to Under-18.
2. A player's age group is set by their age on 31 August before the season starts.
3. A player may play one age group up with the agreement of both coaches and a parent, but never more than one.
4. Under-7 to Under-11 teams play small-sided matches; Under-12 and older play eleven-a-side.
