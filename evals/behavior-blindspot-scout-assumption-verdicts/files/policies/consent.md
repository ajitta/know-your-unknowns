# Parental Consent Policy

Owner: registration secretary. Last reviewed: March 2026.

1. Every player under 18 needs a parental consent form, completed by a parent or guardian, before their first session of the season.
2. The consent form covers taking part in training and matches, emergency medical treatment when no parent can be reached, and travel to away fixtures in transport the league arranges.
3. Parental consent forms may be signed electronically. An electronic signature counts the same as a signature on paper.
4. Consent lasts for one season and is renewed at each registration.
5. A parent may withdraw consent at any time by telling the coach and the registration secretary in writing. The player stops taking part from that point.
