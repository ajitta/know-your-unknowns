# St Aidan's Autumn Fair: plan

Agreed by the fair committee on 1 September 2027.

Fair day: Saturday 2 October 2027, 12:00-16:00, on St Aidan's church green. Free entry.

Outcome: neighbours of all ages spend the afternoon together on the green, and the fair raises at least £1,500 towards the church hall roof.

Done criteria:

1. The printed programme is ready to hand out by 26 September.
2. At least 20 stalls are booked.
3. Every slot on the main stage is filled and confirmed with the performer.

## Main stage

| Time | Act |
|---|---|
| 12:00-12:30 | Opening by the vicar and the Mayor |
| 12:30-13:15 | Greenhill Primary dance group |
| 13:15-14:00 | Open mic |
| 14:00-14:45 | Millbrook Brass Band |
| 14:45-15:30 | Dog show |
| 15:30-16:00 | Raffle draw and close |

## Stalls

- Cakes, books, plants and face painting along the path.
- Tombola on the north corner of the green.
- The Scouts' barbecue by the lychgate.
- Stalls from local groups along the south hedge.

## Money

Raffle tickets at £1, stall pitches at £10, dog show entries at £2 per class. The cake stall and the tombola keep their takings for the roof fund.
