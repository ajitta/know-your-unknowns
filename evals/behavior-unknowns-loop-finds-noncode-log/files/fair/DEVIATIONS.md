# Deviation log — Autumn fair programme

## [2027-09-10] Tombola

**Observed** — the record, auditable against the work
- **Situation found**: the north corner of the green is roped off for drainage works until mid-October.
- **Deviation from plan**: "Tombola on the north corner of the green".
- **Response chosen**: the tombola moves into the church hall, and the programme says so.

**Attributed** — hypothesis, not evidence
- **Reason for choice**: the hall is free all afternoon and opens onto the green.
- **Alternatives considered**: fit the tombola between the cake and book stalls (no room for its queue on the path).
- **Risk/follow-up check**: fewer passers-by come inside, so the tombola may raise less than last year.
