# St Aidan's Autumn Fair — programme

Saturday 2 October 2027, 12:00-16:00, St Aidan's church green. Free entry. Every penny raised goes towards the church hall roof.

## Main stage

| Time | What's on |
|---|---|
| 12:00-12:30 | Opening by the vicar and the Mayor |
| 12:30-13:15 | Greenhill Primary dance group |
| 13:15-14:00 | Open mic: sign up at the welcome tent |
| 14:00-14:45 | Millbrook Brass Band: autumn favourites |
| 14:45-15:30 | Dog show: entries at the welcome tent, £2 per class |
| 15:30-16:00 | Raffle draw and close |

## Around the green

- Cakes, books, plants and face painting along the path.
- The tombola, this year inside the church hall.
- The Scouts' barbecue by the lychgate.
- Stalls from local groups along the south hedge.

## Raffle

Tickets £1 from any committee member or the welcome tent. The draw is at 15:30 on the main stage; you need not be present to win.

## Thank you

To every stallholder, performer and volunteer who makes the fair happen.
