## 4. Orders

4.1 The Buyer places Orders through its purchasing system or by email.

4.2 An Order is binding once the Supplier confirms it, or 2 working days after it is sent if the Supplier has not rejected it by then.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 The Supplier delivers to the Store named in the Order within @LEAD@ working days of confirming it, between 07:00 and 11:00 unless the Store agrees otherwise.

5.2 Risk in the Goods passes to the Buyer on delivery. Title passes when the Buyer has paid for them.

## 6. Prices

6.1 Prices are those in the Supplier's price list in force on the date of the Order.

6.2 The Supplier gives the Buyer at least 60 days' written notice of a price increase, and increases its prices no more than once in any 12-month period.

## 7. Payment

7.1 The Buyer pays each valid invoice within 30 days of the date it receives it.

7.2 The Supplier sends invoices by email to the Buyer's accounts payable mailbox, quoting the Order number. An invoice without an Order number is not a valid invoice.

7.3 The Buyer may change the payment terms in clause 7.1 by giving the Supplier at least 30 days' written notice.

## 8. Quality and returns

8.1 The Goods match their description in the Supplier's price list and are fit for sale to retail customers or, for services, are performed with reasonable skill and care.

8.2 The Buyer may return Goods that do not meet clause 8.1 within 14 days of delivery, and the Supplier credits their price in full.

## 9. Liability

Neither party is liable for loss of profit or for indirect loss. Each party's total liability in any contract year is limited to the amount the Buyer paid under this Agreement in that year, except for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

## 10. Termination

10.1 Either party may end this Agreement at once by written notice if the other commits a material breach and does not remedy it within 30 days of being asked to.

10.2 Orders confirmed before termination are delivered and paid for under this Agreement.

## 11. Notices

Notices under this Agreement are given in writing, by email to the contacts in the Schedule or by post to each party's registered office.

## 12. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
