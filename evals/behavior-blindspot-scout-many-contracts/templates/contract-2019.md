## 4. Orders

4.1 Orders are placed by the Buyer by email or through its purchasing system.

4.2 An Order becomes binding on confirmation by the Supplier, or 2 working days after it is sent unless rejected by the Supplier within that time.

## 5. Delivery

5.1 Delivery is made to the Store named in the Order within @LEAD@ working days of confirmation, between 07:00 and 11:00 unless otherwise agreed with the Store.

5.2 Risk passes to the Buyer on delivery. Title passes on payment in full.

## 6. Prices

6.1 Prices are as set out in the Supplier's price list current at the date of the Order.

6.2 Price increases require not less than 60 days' written notice to the Buyer and may be made no more than once in any 12-month period.

## 7. Payment

7.1 The Buyer pays each valid invoice within 30 days of the date it receives it.

7.2 Invoices are sent by email to the Buyer's accounts payable mailbox and must quote the Order number. An invoice that does not quote the Order number is not a valid invoice.

7.3 The payment terms in clause 7.1 may be changed only by a written amendment signed by both parties.

## 8. Quality and returns

8.1 The Goods shall conform to their description in the Supplier's price list and be fit for retail sale or, in the case of services, be performed with reasonable skill and care.

8.2 Non-conforming Goods may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full credit.

## 9. Liability

Neither party shall be liable for loss of profit or for indirect or consequential loss. Liability in any contract year is limited to the sums paid by the Buyer under this Agreement in that year, save for death or personal injury caused by negligence.

## 10. Termination

10.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the other is in material breach and fails to remedy the breach within 30 days of a written request to do so.

10.2 Orders confirmed before termination shall be fulfilled and paid for under this Agreement.

## 11. Notices

Notices shall be in writing and sent by email to the contacts in the Schedule or by post to the registered office of the party concerned.
