# Procurement Policy

Owner: Finance Director. Last reviewed: January 2026.

1. Every supplier of goods or services that the Stores sell or use has a signed supplier agreement before the first order. Agreements are filed in contracts/ under their contract number.
2. New agreements use the current template, GC-SUP-2022. Agreements on earlier templates stay in force until they are replaced or end.
3. The Purchasing Manager may agree changes to delivery days, lead times and price lists.
4. Any change to the payment terms, the term or the liability of an agreement with an estimated annual spend above £50,000 needs the Finance Director's written approval before it is sent to the supplier.
5. New suppliers are checked against policies/supplier-onboarding.md before their agreement is signed.
6. The Purchasing Manager reviews agreements every two years and records the review in notes/.
