# Fair Payment Pledge

Signed by the Managing Director on 3 March 2022, and shown on our website and at every Store's customer service desk.

We pledge to our suppliers:

1. Suppliers with fewer than 50 staff are paid within 30 days of a valid invoice, and are never moved to longer terms.
2. We tell a supplier within 5 working days if we dispute an invoice, and pay the part we do not dispute on time.
3. We talk to a supplier before we change its payment terms.

We are listed in the Garden Retail Suppliers' Forum register of pledge signatories. A complaint the Forum upholds removes us from the register.
