# Supplier Onboarding

Owner: Purchasing Manager. Last reviewed: September 2025.

Before a new supplier's agreement is signed:

1. Check the company number and registered office at Companies House.
2. Confirm the bank details by phone with a named contact, never from an email alone.
3. Get a current public liability insurance certificate (at least £5 million).
4. Cafe food and drink suppliers: a food hygiene rating of 4 or 5, and the supplier's food safety statement.
5. Live fish and plant suppliers: plant passport registration where it applies.
6. Add the supplier to the stock system with its lead time and delivery days.
7. File the signed agreement in contracts/ and send the supplier a copy.
