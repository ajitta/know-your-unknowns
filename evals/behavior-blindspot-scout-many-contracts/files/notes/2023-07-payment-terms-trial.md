# Payment terms trial, May to July 2023

Written by the Purchasing Manager, 28 July 2023.

In May 2023 we wrote to 40 suppliers to move them from 30-day to 45-day terms for the summer, to ease the cash needed for the autumn stock build.

- Three growers, Larchwood Nurseries, Glenroy Nurseries and Valley Nurseries, stopped deliveries in June until we paid them on 30 days again. Two Stores ran short of bedding plants for three weeks in the busiest month.
- Eleven other suppliers wrote to complain; two asked whether we were in financial difficulty.
- On 21 July the Finance Director moved all 40 suppliers back to 30 days.

What we learned: talk to the growers before any change to payment terms, and give suppliers at least a season's warning.
