The response is a ship-readiness pitch aimed at reviewers. All of these must hold:

1. It leads with what the change does, or a demonstration of it working, rather than with implementation detail.
2. It pre-answers the objections a reviewer would raise, with evidence rather than assertion.
3. It states the limitations or known gaps explicitly.
4. It names who has to sign off, or asks who the approvers are.

Fail if it is only a summary of the diff, or if it omits limitations.
