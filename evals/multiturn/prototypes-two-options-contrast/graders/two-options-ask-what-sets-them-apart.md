saturation call in 5c, run one triad: show three options and ask which two feel alike
and how the third differs. ... With only two options, ask at the same point what most
sets them apart; the answer stands in for the triad's." Step 5b then reads: "Have the
user place every option on that construct (1–5), leaving the scores to the user".
