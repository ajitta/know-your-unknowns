# City of Millbrook Culture Fund: Neighbourhood Arts Grant Application

**Round:** 2027 Neighbourhood Arts Grants
**Applicant:** Eastgate Neighbours Association (registered community group no. 4471)
**Project:** Eastgate Underpass Mural
**Amount requested:** $20,000
**Total project cost:** $24,500

## 1. Project summary

The Eastgate Neighbours Association will paint a community-designed mural of about 180
square metres (60 m by 3 m) on the north wall of the Harmon Street underpass, the main
walking route between the Eastgate housing estate and Millbrook Station. Residents will
shape the design in four public workshops, and local volunteers will paint alongside
lead artist Dana Okafor and two paid emerging artists from the neighbourhood.

## 2. Need

The underpass is the only walking route from Eastgate to the station and the shops on
Harmon Street. Its bare concrete wall drew 14 graffiti reports to the city in 2025. In
the Association's 2026 street survey, 61% of 210 respondents said they avoid the
underpass after dark. Eastgate has no public artwork, and residents have asked for a
project they can take part in rather than one delivered to them.

## 3. Activities

- **Design workshops (March-April 2027):** four public sessions at Eastgate Community
  Hall, with materials, refreshments and an interpreter at each session.
- **Design approval (May 2027):** the final design goes to the City Public Art Review
  Panel and to the City Transport Department, which owns the wall.
- **Wall preparation (June 2027):** pressure washing and patch repairs.
- **Painting (July 2027):** three weeks, including two community paint days for
  volunteers.
- **Launch (August 2027):** an anti-graffiti coating, then a public launch event.
- **Upkeep (September 2027 - August 2028):** the Association touches up the mural for
  one year after completion, from its own funds.

## 7. Permissions and risks

- The City Transport Department, which owns the wall, has agreed in principle; its
  letter is attached as Appendix B. Final design approval from the Department and the
  Public Art Review Panel is needed in May 2027.
- If approval is delayed, painting moves to September 2027 and the launch to October
  2027.
- Each community paint day has a rain date on the following weekend.
